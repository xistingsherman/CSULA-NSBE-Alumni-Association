About us

Our mission

To connect generations of Cal State LA NSBE alumni, preserving our legacy and honoring the contributions of Black engineers and technologists.

Who we are

One starting point, many careers

The Cal State LA NSBE Alumni Association is the home for alumni of the National Society of Black Engineers (NSBE) chapter at Cal State LA, and a welcoming community for Black engineering and technology alumni of Cal State LA. We are engineers, technologists, researchers, founders and educators who share one starting point: the classrooms, labs and late nights at Cal State LA.

Our members span many graduating classes and careers. What connects us is a shared history, and a commitment to making sure that history is remembered.

Why we exist

So no part of our legacy is lost

For years, Cal State LA NSBE alumni have gone on to do remarkable work, often without their classmates, their chapter or the next generation ever hearing about it. After graduation, many of us lost touch. Our stories went undocumented.

We believe every graduate deserves to be celebrated, and every alumnus deserves a place to stay connected.

This association exists so that no part of our legacy is lost.

What we do

How we keep our community connected

Share our stories

Through alumni interviews and our newsletter, we record the careers, milestones and achievements of our members.

Honor achievement

Each year, our Alumni Recognition Award celebrates an alumnus whose work has made a lasting impact in engineering or technology.

Welcome new alumni

Our annual graduate banquet celebrates each graduating class of Black engineers and welcomes them into the alumni network.

Stay connected

Through gatherings, mailings and a growing directory, we help alumni across generations find each other again.

Join us

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Who can join?

Our association is rooted in Cal State LA NSBE and centers Black engineering and technology alumni. However, membership is open to any Cal State LA engineering or technology alum who supports our mission.

I wasn't in NSBE at Cal State LA. Can I still join?

Yes. If you're a Cal State LA engineering or technology alum who supports our mission, you're welcome here, whether or not you were a chapter member. And it's never too late to join NSBE: membership is open to professionals at any stage of their career.

Is this an official NSBE chapter?

No. We are an independent alumni association for Cal State LA NSBE alumni. We share NSBE's values, but we are not a chartered NSBE professional chapter.

Do I need to live in Los Angeles?

No. Our alumni live across the country, and most of our work happens online and by mail.

How can I share my story or nominate someone?

Email us at info@csulansbealumni.org to tell us about your career, a milestone or a fellow alum we should recognize.

Are current students involved?

Our focus is alumni. Each year we celebrate graduating students at our banquet and welcome them into the alumni network.

How can I get more involved?

We're always looking for alumni to help with interviews, events, the newsletter and the banquet. Email us at info@csulansbealumni.org.
