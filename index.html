Who we are

A home base for Cal State LA NSBE graduates

The Cal State LA NSBE Alumni Association brings together graduates of the Cal State LA chapter of the National Society of Black Engineers. Whether you graduated last spring or decades ago, this is your network.

We carry forward NSBE's mission: to increase the number of culturally responsible Black engineers who excel academically, succeed professionally, and positively impact the community.

  • Reconnect with classmates and meet alumni across generations
  • Advance careers through networking, referrals, and peer mentoring
  • Celebrate alumni achievements and milestones
  • Keep our community strong for every graduating class

What we do

Programs

Peer Mentorship

Alumni-to-alumni mentoring for career changes, promotions, grad school, and starting a business.

Socials & Reunions

Mixers, homecoming reunions, and family-friendly gatherings to keep the community close.

Career Network

Job leads, referrals, and a private member directory to connect across engineering, tech, and beyond.

Professional Development

Workshops on leadership, licensure, grad school, entrepreneurship, and moving up in your career.
